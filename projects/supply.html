Overview

An operations dashboard covering shipments, suppliers and inventory across warehouses. It replaces three Excel trackers with a single view the operations team checks every morning.

Methodology

Step 01

Data Preparation

Consolidated shipment, purchase order and stock-level trackers from Excel into SQL tables with consistent supplier and SKU keys.

Step 02

Analytical Approach

Measured on-time rate and lead time per supplier, and flagged SKUs below reorder point against their average daily demand.

Step 03

Visual Design

Exceptions first: late shipments and low-stock SKUs are highlighted, with drill-through to supplier and warehouse detail.

Dashboard pages

Page 01 Page 02 Page 03

Key findings

  1. 1

    Two suppliers account for most late deliveries; the rest consistently deliver on time.

  2. 2

    Stockouts cluster in the weeks after long supplier lead times, not after demand spikes.

  3. 3

    One warehouse holds excess stock of slow movers while another runs short on the same SKUs.

Recommendations

  • ›

    Renegotiate SLAs — or add backup suppliers — for the two least reliable suppliers.

  • ›

    Set reorder points from actual lead time per supplier instead of a single default.

  • ›

    Rebalance stock between warehouses weekly using the excess/shortage view.
