Overview

A three-page sales report for a multi-region retailer. It replaces a weekly spreadsheet pack with one place to see revenue, orders and margin — and drill from region to category to product.

Methodology

Step 01

Data Preparation

Wrote SQL views joining orders, products, customers and regions; handled returns and refunds so revenue and margin reconcile with finance.

Step 02

Analytical Approach

DAX measures for revenue, margin, AOV and year-over-year change. Category and region contribution analysis to separate volume from value.

Step 03

Visual Design

Overview page for leadership, a detail table for category managers, and a breakdown page for regional teams — one consistent filter set across all three.

Key findings

  1. 1

    Electronics drives the most revenue but runs below-average margin — growth there is costing profit.

  2. 2

    Two regions account for over half of revenue; the rest are flat year over year.

  3. 3

    Average order value is stable, so growth is coming from more orders, not bigger baskets.

  4. 4

    A small group of slow-moving SKUs ties up inventory with almost no revenue contribution.

Recommendations

  • ›

    Review pricing and supplier terms on high-volume, low-margin Electronics lines.

  • ›

    Test basket-building promotions to lift AOV rather than discounting for volume.

  • ›

    Rationalise the bottom-performing SKUs and redirect shelf space to proven sellers.

  • ›

    Set region-level targets based on the contribution analysis, not last year plus a flat percentage.
