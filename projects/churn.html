Overview

A churn analysis for a subscription telecom business. Python handles cleaning and segmentation; Power BI turns it into a view the retention team can act on each week.

Methodology

Step 01

Data Preparation

Cleaned and typed the customer table in Pandas, fixed blank charges for new accounts, and binned tenure into cohorts.

Step 02

Analytical Approach

Compared churn rates across contract, tenure, payment method and support history; flagged at-risk customers with simple, explainable rules.

Step 03

Visual Design

One overview, one segment table, one breakdown page — every chart answers 'who is leaving and why'.

Key findings

  1. 1

    Month-to-month customers churn at roughly three times the rate of annual contracts.

  2. 2

    Churn is concentrated in the first year of tenure; customers who pass 12 months mostly stay.

  3. 3

    Customers with multiple support tickets churn far more often than price alone would predict.

  4. 4

    Price differences explain less churn than expected once contract type is accounted for.

Recommendations

  • ›

    Offer an incentive to move month-to-month customers onto annual contracts in their first 90 days.

  • ›

    Build a first-year onboarding check-in for new customers.

  • ›

    Route customers with repeat support tickets to a retention specialist.

  • ›

    Review the at-risk list weekly and track save rate as a KPI.
