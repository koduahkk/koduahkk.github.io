Customer Churn Analysis

Everyone assumed price drove churn. The data said contract type and support tickets did. A Python and Power BI breakdown of who leaves, when, and what to do about it.

Role
Solo analyst: Python, analysis, design
Type
Portfolio project
Tools
Python · pandas · Power BI
Dataset
7,043 customers
01 —The question

What did the business need to know?

If we cut prices, will fewer people leave?

A subscription telecom business was losing customers and the working theory was price. Before cutting prices, the retention team needed to know who actually leaves, when, and why.

02 —The data

What I had to work with.

One customer-level table covering contract, tenure, charges, payment method, services and support history.

03 —The approach

From raw tables to one trusted model.

  1. Step 01

    Clean and type in pandas

    Fixed blank charges for new accounts, cast types and binned tenure into cohorts.

    Python: clean & segment+ 
    import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv("customers.csv")

# Blank total_charges = brand-new accounts with no bill yet
df["total_charges"] = pd.to_numeric(df["total_charges"], errors="coerce").fillna(0)
df["churned"] = df["churn"].eq("Yes")

df["tenure_cohort"] = pd.cut(df["tenure"], bins=[0, 12, 24, 48, 72],
                             labels=["0-12m", "13-24m", "25-48m", "49-72m"], include_lowest=True)

churn_by_contract = (df.groupby("contract")["churned"]
                       .mean().mul(100).round(1).sort_values(ascending=False))
  2. Step 02

    Compare, then control

    Compared churn across contract, tenure, payment method and support history, then checked whether price still mattered once contract type was held constant.

  3. Step 03

    Flag who’s at risk

    Simple, explainable rules the retention team can act on weekly, with no black box.

04 —The insights
Finding 01

Month-to-month customers churn at about 3× the rate.

Contract type explains more churn than any price band. Once contract is held constant, price differences matter far less than expected.

Finding 02

The first year is the danger zone.

Churn concentrates in the first 12 months of tenure. Customers who pass a year mostly stay.

05 —The impact

What changed, and what I recommended.

1,120at-risk customers flagged for outreach
3×churn on monthly plans
Year 1highest-risk period
  1. Offer an incentive to move month-to-month customers onto annual contracts in their first 90 days.
  2. Build a first-year onboarding check-in, and route repeat support-ticket customers to a retention specialist.
  3. Review the at-risk list weekly and track save rate as a KPI.
