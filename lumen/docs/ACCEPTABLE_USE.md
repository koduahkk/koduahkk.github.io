# Acceptable use (draft for the Terms of Service)

> Draft wording for counsel to review. The onboarding "Using Lumen
> responsibly" step links to the published version of this text, and users must
> accept it (versioned as `CURRENT_TERMS_VERSION` in `lumen-core`) before they
> can capture anything.

You may not use Lumen:

1. During exams, quizzes, proctored or timed assessments, certification tests,
   or any other evaluation where outside help or AI assistance is not allowed.
2. During job interviews or hiring assessments, unless the interviewer has
   explicitly allowed AI tools.
3. To record or transcribe people without telling them, or anywhere recording
   without everyone's consent is against the law. Laws vary; in some places
   everyone in a conversation must agree to be recorded.
4. To capture information you are not authorised to access, or to get around
   any security, monitoring or proctoring software.

Lumen is designed to be visible. It shows a capture indicator whenever it reads
your screen or audio, it never hides itself from screen sharing or recording,
and it reminds you to tell other participants when it hears meeting audio.
Accounts that break these rules may be suspended or closed.
